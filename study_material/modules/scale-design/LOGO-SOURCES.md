# Technology logo sources

The `logo-*.svg` files are self-hosted copies of the matching brand paths from
[Simple Icons v16.35.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.35.0/icons).
Each filename matches the source icon slug; for example, `logo-nginx.svg` came from
[the pinned NGINX SVG](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.35.0/icons/nginx.svg).
The CDN reported `x-jsd-version: 16.35.0` at download time.
The paths are colored for contrast in the learning tables; their geometry is unchanged.
Simple Icons is CC0-1.0, but individual brand marks may have their own trademark
or usage rules. The marks identify the products discussed, not sponsorship.
The source URLs are the provenance for each filename.
